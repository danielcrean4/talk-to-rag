# Tides

## What causes them

The sea rises and falls twice a day because the Moon pulls on the water nearest to it more strongly than on the water farthest away. The Sun does the same, with a little under half the Moon's effect.

## Spring and neap tides

When the Sun, Earth and Moon are in a line, at new moon and full moon, the two pulls add up and the range between high and low water is largest. These are spring tides. When the Moon is at a right angle to the Sun, at the quarter moons, the pulls partly cancel and the range is smallest. These are neap tides.

## Local differences

The shape of a coast changes the tide far more than the Moon's distance does. A funnel-shaped bay concentrates the incoming water, which is why some bays have a range of over ten metres while an open ocean island may see less than one.
